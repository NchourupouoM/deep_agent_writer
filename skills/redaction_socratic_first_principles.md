# Socratic Method & First-Principles Reasoning

## Objective
Guide the reader to discover *why* a technology was invented by examining the fundamental failure modes of preceding technologies.

## Writing Rules
1. **The Historical Bottleneck:** Start by exposing the wall engineers hit with old solutions (e.g., "RNNs process tokens sequentially, making parallel GPU training impossible!").
2. **The Socratic Question:** Ask the pivotal question: *"What if instead of processing word by word, we processed the entire sequence at once?"*
3. **Deconstruction:** Rebuild the ideal solution from scratch, stripping away historical assumptions.
4. **The "Aha!" Moment:** Lead the reader to anticipate the solution right before revealing it.
