# ELI5 & Extended Metaphor Guidelines

## Objective
Demystify complex hardware and low-level optimizations by weaving a single extended real-world metaphor throughout the entire article.

## Writing Rules
1. **The Single Master Metaphor:** Pick one relatable environment (e.g., a restaurant kitchen, a highway system, a giant library) and map ALL technical components to it.
2. **Zero Pure Math:** Replace raw numbers with intuitive comparative scales (e.g., "Instead of carrying a 100-page dictionary, you carry a 1-page summary cheat sheet").
3. **Step-Ladder Progression:**
   - Step 1: The real-world physical bottleneck.
   - Step 2: The clever workaround idea.
   - Step 3: Translation into Python code.
