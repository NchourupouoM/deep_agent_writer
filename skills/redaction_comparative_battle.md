# Head-to-Head Technical Battle Guidelines

## Objective
Provide an impartial, rigorous framework helping engineers choose the exact tool for their specific constraints.

## Writing Rules
1. **Initial Comparison Matrix:** Open immediately with a summary matrix evaluating Ease of Use, Speed, Cost, and Ideal Use Case.
2. **Round-by-Round Breakdown:**
   - Round 1: Implementation Simplicity & DX.
   - Round 2: Performance & Scalability.
   - Round 3: Ecosystem, Maintenance & Production Readiness.
3. **Engineer's Verdict:** Conclude with a clear decision rule: *"Choose X if your priority is A; Choose Y if your constraint is B."*
