# Excalidraw Specification - Vector Space & Geometric Intuition

## Visual Directives
- **Layout:** 2D or isometric 3D coordinate system (X and Y axes with hand-drawn style).
- **Vectors & Points:**
  - Semantically similar embeddings (e.g., "King", "Queen"): Nearby points connected by green dashed distance lines.
  - Angle & Distance: Arc labeled with $\theta$ representing Cosine Similarity metric.
- **Labels:** Hand-drawn text callouts annotating vector axes (e.g., "Gender Direction Vector", "Royalty Axis").
