# Feynman Technique Pedagogical Guidelines

## Objective
Enable a complete beginner to grasp the deep intuition behind complex AI theory without getting lost in mathematical abstractions.

## Writing Rules
1. **Real-World Analogy First:** ALWAYS open with a familiar physical scenario before introducing formal definitions.
2. **Jargon Deconstruction:** Never introduce a technical term without immediately translating it into plain English.
3. **First-Principles Breakdown:** Break down mathematical equations variable by variable (e.g., Query, Key, Value vectors explained via a library search analogy).
4. **Self-Testing Question:** End each major section with a simple conceptual question and answer it step by step.
